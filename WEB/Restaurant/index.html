Welcome to Aeterna

"A Modern European Sanctuary in the Golden City."
Nestled within the historic walls of Old Town Prague, Aeterna is an exclusive dining sanctuary founded on the pursuit of the timeless supper. Our restaurant emerged from the vision of Chef Julian Martel, who sought to fuse the classic precision of European cuisine with a philosophy unburdened by fleeting trends.
Established in a meticulously restored 19th-century ballroom, Aeterna's origin is rooted in the city's rich architectural heritage. Every dish is a reflection of seasonal abundance, transforming local Czech ingredients and techniques from the Mediterranean coast into lasting culinary memories. We invite you to experience an evening where the grand tradition of Prague meets the art of modern, intimate dining.

Tasting Menus

Whether you choose our signature journey or the ultimate expression of our artistry, your supper will be a timeless narrative.

The Cellar Legacy

The Aeterna wine program is a meticulous reflection of our culinary philosophy: rooted in European heritage and driven by timeless quality. We view the wine list not merely as an inventory, but as a living testament to the history of great vineyards.

Our collection is an extensive, carefully curated portfolio emphasizing rare Central European vintages and established French maisonsbottles selected not by trend, but by their ability to achieve perfect synergy with Chef Martel's cuisine. Guided by our Sommelier, every glass poured is an elevation of the dining narrative. We invite you to explore this legacy.

The Chef Legacy

A Five-Course Masterpiece. €210 per guest.

Starters

The Foie Gras Landscape

Terrine of seared Foie Gras with black fig jam, served on toasted brioche with a balsamic reduction and sea salt crystals.

Smoked Eel Tartare

Delicate smoked eel tartare with green apple ribbons, wasabi pearls, and a lime and soy emulsion.

Heirloom Tomato Consommé

Clear, cold consommé of heirloom tomatoes, clarified with basil essence, served with burrata mousse and tomato skin crisps.

Beetroot & Goat Cheese Sphere

An edible sphere of goat cheese, beetroot purée, and toasted walnuts, finished with a fresh dill oil.

Main Courses

Octopus and Chorizo Powder

Charred octopus tentacle served with potato foam, smoked paprika oil, and a dusting of dry cured Iberian chorizo powder.

Duck Breast & Honey Glaze

Pan-seared duck breast with a local acacia honey glaze, paired with parsnip purée and pickled cherries.

Turbot with Champagne Sauce

Fillet of wild turbot cooked sous-vide, served with a delicate Champagne-and-chive butter sauce and sea bean garnish.

Veal Cheek & Celery Root

Slow braised veal cheek in red wine and bone marrow reduction, served with roasted celery root and microgreens

Desserts

Pistachio and Raspberry Tower

Layers of pistachio sponge, raspberry gélee, and white chocolate mousse, finished with a mirror glaze.

Caribbean Rum Baba

A classic Baba cake soaked in aged dark rum syrup, served warm with vanilla bean ice cream and candied orange peel.

The Lemon Verbena Mousse

Light and airy lemon verbena mousse, served in a chocolate cage with passionfruit coulis and a basil micro-sponge.

Main Airports

Austria is globally connected through Vienna International Airport (VIE), the country's largest hub. Other convenient entry points include Salzburg (SZG) and Innsbruck (INN), especially for alpine adventures.

Train Connections

Direct trains are available from major cities in Europe, the US, and Asia. Excellent rail connections (ÖBB) also link Austria with neighboring countries like Germany, Switzerland, and Italy.

Visa Access

As part of the Schengen Area, most EU citizens and travelers from visa-exempt countries (like the USA, UK, or Canada) can enter for up to 90 days. Please check your local embassy for specific requirements.

X-AERIAL: YOUR EDGE

X-AERIAL is more than a resource; it's a movement built for those who live on the edge of the atmosphere. We are the architects of adrenaline, dedicated to showcasing the disciplines that push human limits against the sky.

Here, you will find exclusive access to BASE Jumping techniques, Skydiving mastery, and the history of Wingsuit flight. This space is dedicated to safety, innovation, and pushing the boundaries of human performance.

X-AERIAL is your window into the elite world of competitive extreme flight. Discover the athletes who have redefined what is possible against gravity, and explore our Hall of Fame which honors the greatest minds and most significant milestone moments in the history of aerial sports.

WHAT WE OFFER

BASE Jumping

#Risk #Exit #Adrenaline

BASE involves jumping from fixed objects (Buildings, Antennas, Spans, Earth) and demands impeccable timing and mental fortitude.

Skydiving

#Freefall #Precision #FirstJump

Skydiving offers unparalleled freedom and requires mastery of body flight, canopy control, and safety protocols. The gateway to extreme air sports.

Wingsuit

#Proximity #Glide #Speed

Wingsuit pilots utilize specialized gear to achieve high forward speeds and controlled trajectories often close to terrain.

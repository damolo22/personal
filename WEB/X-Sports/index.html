WHAT WE OFFER

BASE Jumping

#Risk #Exit #Adrenaline

BASE involves jumping from fixed objects (Buildings, Antennas, Spans, Earth) and demands impeccable timing and mental fortitude.

Skydiving

#Freefall #Precision #FirstJump

Skydiving offers unparalleled freedom and requires mastery of body flight, canopy control, and safety protocols. The gateway to extreme air sports.

Wingsuit

#Proximity #Glide #Speed

Wingsuit pilots utilize specialized gear to achieve high forward speeds and controlled trajectories often close to terrain.

MIKE LYNCH

Lynch is redefining canopy control and accuracy. Having shattered records for target landings in extreme mountain environments, he specializes in high-risk BASE jumps. His unique technical approach earned him a key spot on the UAOC Roster.

Vogt is known for his aggressive, high-speed lines and near impossible mountain traverses. Although newer to the circuit, he is rapidly becoming one of the fastest pilots in the proximity category, pushing the limits of human flight and technical gear.
